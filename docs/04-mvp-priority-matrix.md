# EXTREMIS Creator Hub — MVP Priority Matrix

Scale: 1 (low) – 5 (high). "Automation potential" = how little manual
work it needs post-launch.

| Feature | Impact | Revenue potential | Dev effort | SEO potential | Automation potential |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| KD Ratio Calculator | 3 | 2 | 1 | 4 | 5 |
| Valorant Sensitivity Converter | 5 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 5 |
| BGMI Sensitivity Helper | 5 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 5 |
| YouTube Title Generator | 4 | 3 | 2 | 4 | 5 |
| YouTube Description Generator | 4 | 3 | 2 | 4 | 5 |
| Affiliate architecture | 3 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 4 (approval is manual, clicks are automatic) |
| Digital products (templates/packs) | 3 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 3 (product creation is manual, delivery is automatic) |
| Display ads | 2 | 4 | 1 | 1 | 5 (once approved) |
| Premium/subscriptions | 2 | 3 | 4 | 1 | 4 |
| Admin panel | 3 | 1 | 3 | 1 | — (enables founder self-service) |

**Reading this table:** the two sensitivity tools carry the highest
combined impact/SEO score and should be built first among the five —
they target the largest, highest-intent existing search demand. The
title/description generators are next, since they serve the "creator"
persona that later converts into digital-product buyers. The KD
calculator is the cheapest to build and ships alongside them, not
before them. Monetization features (affiliate, products, ads,
premium) are correctly sequenced *after* the tools per the roadmap —
none of them work without traffic to monetize.
